# UK Primary School Maths Guide

An interactive reference for parents helping their children with primary school maths. Covers every key topic from Reception through Year 6, with clear explanations of *why* methods work, expandable detail on key concepts, worked examples, and checkable practice activities.

## What it does

Primary school maths has changed. Children are taught to understand *why* methods work before moving to compact written procedures — which can be confusing for parents who learned differently. This guide bridges that gap.

For each topic you get:

- **Summary** — what the topic is actually about
- **Key points** — the core ideas, with expandable detail explaining the reasoning behind them
- **Examples** — worked through step by step, with more available on demand
- **Teaching idea** — a practical way to explore the concept at home
- **Try it** — three checkable activities to practise with your child

Topics span basic number and place value through to fractions, percentages, geometry, statistics, and early algebra. The Year-by-Year guide gives a roadmap of what children typically learn at each stage from Reception to Year 6.

## Maths Quest (practice for kids)

`/practice/` is a separate, child-facing practice app built with Solid.js. Progress is saved in the browser's local storage.

- **18 topics**, from number bonds to negative numbers, each with 5 levels of randomly generated questions (with hints and worked explanations)
- **Adaptive levels**: 7 of 8 right first time moves up a level; a run of wrong answers moves down
- **Modes**: daily challenge, quick mix, pick a topic, times tables (choose which tables), 60-second Beat the Clock, Fix my mistakes, and Stretch it
- **Stretch it**: simple facts from earlier years made bigger or smaller (×10, ×100, ×1000, tenths, hundredths), e.g. 3 + 7 = 10 → 300 + 700 = 1000 → 0.3 + 0.7 = 1. Also mixed into quick mix and the daily challenge for older children
- **Ways to work it out**: every question can show several methods from the guide (partitioning, counting up, friendly numbers, grid method, chunking, inverse facts, times-table shortcuts…) with number-line, grid, array and fraction-bar pictures. Shown after "Don't know", after a wrong answer, or via "See other ways" after a right one
- **Toolbox**: interactive number line, times grid, 100 square, place value chart, fraction wall and a list of tricks for each topic, usable mid-question with no penalty
- **Rewards**: grown-ups set rewards (e.g. 1000 points = film night); children spend points on them and grown-ups mark them as given
- **Spaced repetition**: topics come back for review after 1–7 days depending on accuracy
- **Weekly points** with an auto goal that rises ~10% on recent weeks, day streaks and badges
- **Grown-ups area** (behind a times-table question): year group, weekly goal, double-points focus topics, level overrides, backup/restore
- **Deep links**: every screen, tool and practice has its own address (hash routes such as `/practice/#/tools/grid`, `/practice/#/play/tables/7,8`, `/practice/#/play/topic/bonds?scale=100`), so they can be bookmarked, added to the Home Screen or messaged. Grown-ups get a list of handy links, and topic/tables/tools screens have a copy-link button
- **Move to another device**: grown-ups can share a transfer link (AirDrop, Messages, email) or show a QR code. The link carries the progress itself, compressed, so nothing is uploaded. Opening it on the new device shows a summary before replacing anything. JSON backup files still work too
- On-screen keypad and large touch targets for iPad and iPhone; can be added to the home screen

## Who it's for

Parents and carers who want to support their child's maths without accidentally undermining what the school is teaching. The aim is not to replace the teacher — it's to help you ask better questions and make maths feel less mysterious at home.
