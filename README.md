# UK Primary School Maths Guide

An interactive reference for parents helping their children with primary school maths. Covers every key topic from Reception through Year 6, with clear explanations of *why* methods work, expandable detail on key concepts, worked examples, and checkable practice activities.

## What it does

Primary school maths has changed. Children are taught to understand *why* methods work before moving to compact written procedures — which can be confusing for parents who learned differently. This guide bridges that gap.

For each topic you get:

- **Summary** — what the topic is actually about
- **Key points** — the core ideas, with expandable detail explaining the reasoning behind them
- **Examples** — worked through step by step, with more available on demand
- **Teaching idea** — a practical way to explore the concept at home
- **Try it** — three checkable activities to practise with your child

Topics span basic number and place value through to fractions, percentages, geometry, statistics, and early algebra. The Year-by-Year guide gives a roadmap of what children typically learn at each stage from Reception to Year 6.

## Who it's for

Parents and carers who want to support their child's maths without accidentally undermining what the school is teaching. The aim is not to replace the teacher — it's to help you ask better questions and make maths feel less mysterious at home.
